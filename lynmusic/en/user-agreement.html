4. Free trial and expiry

The trial is free, lasts 168 hours and never automatically charges you. Each Apple purchasing account may claim it only once across platforms. Reinstallation, restoration, changing devices, refunds or revocation do not create another claim opportunity. Claims require eligibility and product checks. You decide whether to buy lifetime access after expiry.

If the trial expires or verification confirms revocation, and no other product continues to cover the current platform:

  • iOS premium themes revert to Classic. New lyrics-image sharing or saving and artwork-style changes are unavailable; your existing artwork style remains.
  • An already enabled advanced player may continue until the next app launch. Without valid access then, it uses automatic mode. Closing and reopening a window is not an app restart. After you switch back to automatic mode, advanced mode cannot be enabled again without access.
  • An advanced player not already enabled cannot be newly enabled. An already displayed system share sheet or submitted photo-library write is not withdrawn.

Regaining access allows the features again, without automatically switching players or repeating earlier actions. The app uses valid local entitlements first and updates them through Apple. With no valid cache and verification still pending, free features may temporarily apply.

An in-app reminder may appear once each at 48 and 24 hours remaining. There are no background system notifications; returning shows only the applicable stage, with no reminders after expiry. Reminders depend on app state; refer to the displayed expiry time.
